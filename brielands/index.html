The cheesiest survival game

Everything is cheese: the trees are breadsticks, the rocks are aged cheddar, the river is nacho sauce, and you are a very worried wedge.

🔥 Don't melt

Fire keeps you warm... until you droop, drip and puddle. Noon sun softens you. Nights freeze you solid.

🌶️ The nacho river

A river of warm nacho sauce. It heals you, but you come out sticky. Scoop it up to make nacho cement.

👑 The cheese gods

Crafting isn't a menu. The cheese gods greet you by name and judge you: strike, stir and stretch your way to Blessed gear.

⚒️ The Fondue Forge

Melt curds in a fondue cauldron, hammer them on a pastry slab, quench in ghee, cave-age the best. Two hands, two weapons.

🧱 Build anything

Cheddar blocks go anywhere (and melt near fire). Nacho cement sets rock hard and glues them together.

🌍 An endless world

Whey Marsh, Parmesan Peaks, the Moldwoods, Fondue Fields and bubbling Fondue Calderas. Keep walking. It keeps going.

💰 Brie City

Buy land, run farms and fondue restaurants, sell Blessed blades at the forge shop. Beat Count Camembert and the copycat rats. Become THE BIG CHEESE.

Fight your friends

Bring the gear from your adventure: your best weapon, your shield, your armour. Then see whose cheese is sharper.

⚔️ Arenas

Team Cheddar vs Team Bleu

  • 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 or 4v4
  • Quick match with random players, or a private room code for your friends
  • Three arenas: Cracker Colosseum, Fondue Pit (fall in, you melt), Whey Marsh
  • The host picks rounds, time, health, gear and friendly fire

🧀 Free-for-all world

Up to 16 cheeses in the real Curdlands. Everyone vs everyone.

  • Team up with friends... until you don't
  • Nacho Cups, Golden Graters and Mozzarella Boots to grab
  • KO streaks and the Big Cheese crown
  • Quick chat: GG! Nice hit! Say cheese!

How to play

Keep your temperature in the green: cheese is comfy at 10 °Ré. Too hot and you melt, too cold and you crack.

WASD move
Space jump
Shift roll like a cheese wheel
L-click left hand
R-click right hand
X swap hands
1-9 pick a tool
E use / talk
Tab crafting (the cheese gods)
F eat
Q throw
Esc pause
